Problem Formulation
Clinical Background & Project Motivation
The Clinical Challenge
  • Progressive Neurodegenerative Disorder: Parkinson's Disease (PD) impairs motor and cognitive faculties in >10 million individuals globally due to dopamine depletion.
  • Vocal Biomarkers in Early Stages: Up to 90% of patients develop hypophonia (reduced loudness), vocal tremor, and dysarthria before motor symptoms appear.
  • Bottlenecks of Current Exams: Traditional clinical evaluations (e.g. UPDRS) require specialized neurologists, are subjective, expensive, and unavailable in rural healthcare.
  • Urgent Need for Early Screening: Early non-invasive detection allows therapeutic interventions that substantially delay progression.
Our Engineering Solution
  • Non-Invasive Acoustic Screening: Analyze sustained vowel /a/ recordings to isolate vocal fold irregularities objectively.
  • Rigorous Paper Replication: Replicate Bukhari & Ogudo (2024) utilizing SMOTE class balancing, 6-component PCA, and an AdaBoost ensemble.
  • Production-Grade Architecture: Modular Python codebase with automated training (train.py), standalone inference (predict.py), and deterministic reproducibility.
  • Tele-Health Deployment: Foundational computational backend for an accessible, low-cost diagnostic web platform.

1. Time-Frequency Fading & MFCCs

Captures vocal tract resonance envelopes, formant bandwidths, and spectral energy shifts across critical frequency bands reflecting articulatory agility.

2. Wavelet Transform Features (WTF)

Multi-resolution time-frequency decomposition identifying micro-tremors, phonatory transients, and localized non-stationary disturbances in speech signals.

3. Vocal Fold Features (VFF / Jitter & Shimmer)

Measures cycle-to-cycle frequency perturbations (jitter) and amplitude variations (shimmer) in glottal pulses, characterizing vocal fold closure instability.

4. TWQT, DFA & Non-Linear Complexity

Quantifies aerodynamic voice tremor (TWQT), turbulent noise ratios, Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA), and entropy revealing non-linear dynamics.

Architecture Rationale & Computational Efficiency

1. Why 6 PCA Components? Compresses 754 highly collinear acoustic dimensions by 99.2% while retaining 41.82% cumulative variance, preventing weak tree learners from catastrophic overfitting.

2. SMOTE + Scaling Synergy: Standardizing features post-SMOTE balances class priors and ensures high-amplitude wavelet attributes do not disproportionately bias orthogonal PCA projections.

Algorithmic Design
Methodology: AdaBoost Ensemble Classification
Adaptive Boosting (AdaBoost) Principles
  • Sequential Hypothesis Learner Aggregation: Unlike bagging (Random Forests) which trains trees in parallel, AdaBoost constructs weak estimators sequentially, with each learner correcting errors made by prior trees.
  • Iterative Instance Weighting: Instances misclassified in iteration t receive amplified weights, compelling the next tree to focus on borderline diagnostic cases.
  • Weighted Majority Voting Consensus: Final classification aggregates all 500 weak hypotheses weighted by their empirical accuracy (α_t):
    H(x) = sign( Σ α_t * h_t(x) )
Hyperparameter Specifications
  • Base Weak Learner: DecisionTreeClassifier: max_depth = 7. Unlike simple 1-split stumps, depth-7 trees capture intricate non-linear interactions across the 6 PCA acoustic components without overfitting.
  • Ensemble Size: n_estimators = 500: 500 decision trees provide smooth asymptotic convergence and maximum voting stability on unseen test instances.
  • Learning Rate: learning_rate = 1.0: Full shrinkage weight per tree update achieves optimal trade-off between convergence speed and gradient stability.
  • Deterministic Reproducibility: Fixed random_state = 42 across SMOTE, train-test splitting, and decision tree generation.
